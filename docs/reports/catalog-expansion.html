extended.

Twenty new application packages across three waves, taking the curated catalog from 17 to 37. Every entry passed the same hard gates: pinned multi-arch images, no privileged or host-network needs, knowable data placement, backupable state, and a stated identity path.

L1

Co-install

Same appliance, same backup, edge and update lifecycle. Every package gets this.

L2

Shared identity

Authentik OIDC, or a documented local-auth exception where the app cannot do it.

L3

Shared library

One bulk directory mounted into two apps. Declared once, mounted by the owner.

L4

Workflow handoff

One app calls another: request, indexer, downloader, library. Owner-configured.

The Everything apps

Fifty-one apps in HeyPuter/everything, all Vite SPAs that load one line from a CDN — <script src="https://js.puter.com/v2/"> — and talk to a small SDK surface. That line is the seam. Homestead does not fork the repo and does not vendor the AGPL SDK: it pins an upstream commit, applies a one-line patch, and serves its own window.puter in place of the cloud. Everything below was measured from the source tree, not from the README.

1 · Pin

Record the commit SHA and per-app licence in vendor/everything/pin.yaml. No fork, no drift, no 434 MB tree.

2 · Patch

One patch per app swaps the CDN script for ours and adds a CSP that fails closed if anything still reaches out.

3 · Bridge

window.puter in our repo, MIT, served as its own script: auth, env, fs, kv, ui, perms, exit.

4 · Test

Run upstream's own vitest suite against the bridge. If they adopt a new API, their tests go red before ours do.

Licence note: an app with no per-app licence inherits the repository's MIT. Nine apps carry AGPL-3.0 explicitly — 3d, building-design, email, notes, pdf, presentation, spreadsheet, video-studio, whiteboard. Serving a modified AGPL app over the network obliges you to offer its source: publish the pinned SHA and the patch, and the obligation is met.

The nine open questions are closed. Ordering changed as a result: F2 and F4 moved off the Wave 1 path.
