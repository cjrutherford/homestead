What could renumber this

S367 fails its exit check

Track B stops and S368–S381 are released. Nothing in Track A moves.

A package fails its hard gates at slice time

The ID is not reused. The slice is withdrawn, the package is recorded in docs/DEFERRED.md, and the next package moves up.

The Compose generator gains a field

A healthcheck or depends_on field would be its own slice, inserted ahead of S361 if any Wave 3 app needs a readiness gate.

F1 cannot deliver supervised export

Wave 1 reverts to owner-supplied credentials. S342's scope changes; its ID does not.
