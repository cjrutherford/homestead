Set up a tunnel

The tunnel publishes this dashboard and Authentik under your domain. Every public name reaches the appliance's edge, which sends it on; the dashboard is published only once you sign in with Authentik, and on its public name only Authentik signs you in. The tunnel uses the Cloudflare token you stored in the setup Domain step, and finds your account from the names' zone. First you see a plan of every change; nothing changes until you approve it.

Apps you publish under the dashboard's public name, such as Authentik at /apps/auth/, share one web address with the dashboard. A browser treats them as one site, so a flaw in one of those apps could reach your dashboard sign-in in that browser. Homestead accepts this so one name and one certificate cover everything; an app that needs to stand apart, such as Immich, keeps a public name of its own.
