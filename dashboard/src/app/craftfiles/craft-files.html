Craft apps open and save documents in your own Nextcloud.

Craft apps can open and save documents in your own Nextcloud. Homestead does the transfer, so no Nextcloud password is ever sent to a craft app or a browser. Without a link, or with an app set to browser-local, documents stay in the browser.
