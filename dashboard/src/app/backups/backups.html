Nightly backups

One policy backs up every installed app that keeps data, every night. Apps with their own schedule below keep it instead.

Recipients and recovery

Every new backup run is encrypted to the appliance identity and to each owner recipient below. Existing runs are not re-encrypted. The appliance identity lives only on this appliance, so add a recipient whose private key you hold, or export the recovery identity and store it offline.
